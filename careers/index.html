About
Viridian Brands.

Viridian Brands builds and grows consumer health brands across oral care, probiotics, medical nutrition, and digestive health. We develop brands from the formulation up, acquire brands with room to grow, and invest in founders we believe in. Each brand operates as its own company with its own funding, backed by resources a standalone startup rarely has: an experienced operating team, capital, a network of scientists and manufacturers, and a playbook that gets sharper with every brand we build. We launch on Amazon and direct to consumer (DTC), then bring the brands that earn it into retail.
